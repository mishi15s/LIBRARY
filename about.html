About Orchid Library

Orchid Library is a digital sanctuary for book lovers. We offer a curated collection of fiction, science, biographies, and more—designed to inspire, educate, and entertain. Whether you're a casual reader or a lifelong learner, Orchid Library is your cozy corner of the internet.

Mishika

Founder & Student

Mishika is a passionate student and book enthusiast who founded Orchid Library with the vision of creating a welcoming digital space for readers of all ages. She is a student of The Jain International School , Bilaspur. She also learns coding at Codingal. As an avid reader herself, she understands the importance of accessible literature and aims to make reading enjoyable for everyone.

Despite her academic commitments, Mishika dedicates her time to curating the library's collection and ensuring that Orchid Library remains a valuable resource for the community. Her goal is to inspire others to discover the joy of reading and lifelong learning.
